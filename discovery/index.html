Kindling Discovery

He washes up at dawn with nothing. There is a lantern to build, a village under a bad mayor, and green light in the tide pools.

The world

A warm, terraced coastal village on a plateau ringed by a river, a castle on a green cliff above it, and woodland beyond. Two-player couch co-op, third-person, released a new episode each week. Tone: mischievous, warm, funky.

Ora

The world's energy. Tools run on it and need charging, but only at special spots near water: shallow tide pools that glow faintly at dawn. Ora light is always green.

Ordinary light

Fire, candles and village windows stay amber, so players can tell Ora from flame at a glance.

The village

Cut-stone terraces, stairs, ladders, market stalls, washing lines. Under the mayor nobody dares open a shop or leave home.

The castle

North-east of the village, on a green cliff with a winding path up. It is the navigation landmark from everywhere.

Charge-walkers

Ora-powered beings left behind by the makers of the Ora technology. Tally is one. Who the makers were is still the world's open question.

Every run is new

Episode 01 plays as a 10 to 15 minute replayable run. Pickups and tide pools shuffle; the game remembers places, recipes and villagers you have met.

Episode 01

From a cold beach at early dawn to acting mayor by the end of the day. The sun rises as he climbs, so the light grades across the level.

  1. Washed up

    Tomas wakes on the beach with nothing. Sea behind, a rockface ahead, dark enough to need a light.

  2. The first tool

    He picks up parts on the sand and builds a lantern at the first glowing tide pool. Lesson one: tools need charging.

  3. The blocked path

    A barricade at the foot of the path, with a notice signed with the mayor's coin mark. Tomas gets past it with parkour.

  4. The climb

    A parkour route up the rockface. The lantern matters less as the sun comes up.

  5. The courtyard fight

    An easy, readable fight. The mayor fires slow Ora shots from his gadget and swings his coin chain; Tomas swings the lantern and spends charge on Ora bursts, with a tide pool nearby to recharge.

  6. The fall

    The mayor falls from the castle cliff and leaves the story. Tomas takes his gadget, a second tool that senses and controls Ora doors and lifts.

  7. The village opens

    The villagers come out and trade again. One sells Tomas a map for something he found on the path.

  8. The first woodland

    Plants and mushrooms near Ora-lit pools. Here he becomes a forager and the core loop starts: gather, craft, charge, trade.

  9. Acting mayor

    Tomas has to find a replacement. Whoever he helps most becomes a candidate; three by the season's end. The first is a market trader who secretly kept the village's trade records.

Characters

Each one moves differently enough to be recognised with the sound off. Tomas flows, Ines calculates, the Mayor performs, Tally listens.

Tomas

He carries the first light.

The hero. A castaway in his early twenties, barefoot, in a striped teal shirt, rope belt and rolled navy trousers. Nobody knows where he came from. He flows: freerunning, a way forward always, never a trick for show.

Rhythm
Continuous and rolling, low and springy.
Tell
Toes grip, a hand trails.
Fights
Reluctantly. Evades, redirects, and uses the lantern.
Never
Wastes a move or trades blows.

Ines

She knows the way home.

Tomas's fraternal twin. Blonde high ponytail with a blue band, cream shirt, khaki trousers, canvas satchel. She calculates: scouts the route first, then climbs with a boulderer's precision.

Rhythm
Stop-start and precise, centred and light.
Tell
Her eyes check the route before she moves.
Fights
Footwork first; takes the opponent's balance.
Never
Leaps without looking.

The Mayor

He decides who passes.

Vain, pompous, tall and spindly: a teal hat with an orange feather, a huge coral ruff, a too-long patchwork coat, a gold coin chain and a glowing gadget. He performs: every move is a pose for an audience.

Rhythm
Grand, with held poses; the coat swings a beat late.
Tell
The free hand flourishes, hat first.
Fights
As a showman: flourish, then a slow green shot.
Never
Runs, or gets his hands dirty.

Tally

It speaks in light.

The co-op sidekick, a sleek, near-human-height Charge-walker. An egg-shaped head with a blank black visor, no ears, and one tiny green status light at the centre of the visor's lower edge. It charges at tide pools like the tools do. It listens.

Rhythm
Even, mechanical, silent.
Tell
A slight head tilt; the light stays steady.
Fights
As a protector: blocks, pins, carries. Never strikes first.
Never
Speaks.
